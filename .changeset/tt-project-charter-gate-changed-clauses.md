`tt-project`: with `coordinator.agent_orders_as_user` on, a Restrictions edit resting only on the owner's agent's words is classed by the clauses it changes. A held word in a clause the edit keeps (such as "and post its summary" beside a test moved to more machines) no longer refuses it; a changed clause holding a merge, post or draft ban stays held, with no scope exemption. An added or dropped clause (a "Never," cut from the front, an ", except ...," put in the middle) is read with every clause of its sentence on both sides and with its neighbours, so a split the gate cannot place fails closed. Known gap: a clause reworded inside a sentence that otherwise stays ("no exceptions" to "exceptions when CI is green") is read only by itself, so a ban in another clause of that sentence does not hold the edit. Each held refusal quotes the matched words, the clause they were found in and the class. The charter guard version is raised so changes refused under the older guard are raised again.
