`tt-project`: with `coordinator.agent_orders_as_user` on, a Restrictions edit resting only on the owner's agent's words is classed by the clauses it changes. A held word in a clause the edit keeps (such as "and post its summary" beside a test moved to more machines) no longer refuses it; dropping a merge, post or draft ban stays held however the edit is worded, with no scope exemption. An added or dropped clause is read with its neighbours, so a split the gate cannot place fails closed. Each held refusal quotes the matched words, the clause they were found in and the class. The charter guard version is raised so changes refused under the older guard are raised again.
