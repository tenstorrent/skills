An order or obligation no longer closes just because its owner tasks finished when an owner's hand-off leaves the acceptance unmet (a `partial:` or `cells unreported:` summary, a blocked, waiting or failed hand-off). A reply that delivers it and a reply after its owners end both leave it open, and the obligation list shows it as `held open` with the reason. A receipt whose evidence names only such owners, or that such an owner files itself, is refused with the reason. A met continuation of that owner still closes it.
