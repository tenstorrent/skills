`ttp push --retire` on the gh workstation route: only a delete that succeeded answers a new request. A failed one (refused or not run on the workstation) is reported and the request runs again under a new key; its receipt stays as it was. With no local branch and no publish record, the tip to save is read from origin's remote-tracking ref, else the head its task handed off on a shared branch.
