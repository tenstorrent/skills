Make the cost of a phase's evidence a decision the agent takes with numbers. The torch reference is
costed before it runs, may drop layers (never widths) with the reduction stated, and never runs
beside a device timing run; a real-weight two-layer check against the HF model class and a
fixed-batch learning check join the composite gate, so architecture and optimiser bugs are caught
without the expensive trajectory. The step timer's contents are declared once and may not grow. The
plan records the cores a program config launches against the chip's grid, and MFU stays against the
whole grid. Each phase tracks a wall-clock budget. `tt_clock_ladder.py` takes `--shape` and
`--sampler-script`, warms every load before the sampler starts, sets `dst_full_sync_en` and prints
the compute grid and DRAM per chip; `tt_mesh_smoke.py` fills its tensors with signed values so a
dropped or misordered contribution cannot land on a plausible sum.
