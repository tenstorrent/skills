`tt-project`: the coordinator's hourly dollar guard is gone. `budget.hourly_coordinator_usd` is retired (`ttp config` reports it as retired): the always-on session coordinator optimizes its own cost inside the dollar caps, so no amount of self-started coordinator spend defers its wakes or holds, defers or narrows worker runs, and it no longer clips a coordinator turn's raised cap or a new session's seed raise (only the dollar caps do). `ttp status` still shows that spend (`coordinator_1h`). The project and account dollar caps, the budget gate levels, the per-task budgets and the waste and total-spend signals stay hard limits. On a plan, the turn-count and same-evidence signals still catch a looping coordinator; they throttle only the coordinator, not worker dispatch: the gate carries `coord_hold` with the time the trailing hour drops back under the limit, the daemon starts no self-started coordinator turn before then, and one turn afterwards takes every event queued meanwhile. User messages and must-act items (overdue promises and orders, failed claims) still wake it at once. Also, a `ready_not_started` alarm for a task a spend gate held until moments ago (within 5 minutes) waits for one dispatch pass that began after the clear before it wakes the coordinator.
