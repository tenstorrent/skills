`tt-project`: the landing-time stat takes a task's landed time from the commit that reached the push branch (its landing, or the head and superseding commit a supersession names), not from when branch-squash bookkeeping wrote the record. The ETA correction per kind is the trailing 7-day median of actual over the raw estimate (was 14 days), bounded at 0.7-3.0 (was 0.5-3.0) and used from 10 deliveries of a kind on (was 5). The hands-on fix ledger counts a quoted `python3 - <<'EOF'` heredoc that writes nothing, `T=<ttp>; $T <lookup>` lines, `ttp rollout status`, `ttp memory --search` and `:`/`continue`/`break` as read-only lookups, so 'no why recorded' lists only calls that change something.
