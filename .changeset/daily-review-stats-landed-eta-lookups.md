`tt-project`: the landing-time stat dates a task that a supersession or squash carried by the superseding commit, or by its own head once that head was on the push branch, not by when branch-squash bookkeeping wrote the record; push queue landings keep their push time. The ETA correction per kind is the trailing 7-day median of actual over the raw estimate (was 14 days), bounded at 0.7-3.0 (was 0.5-3.0) and used from 10 deliveries of a kind on (was 5). The hands-on fix ledger counts a quoted `python3 - <<'EOF'` heredoc that writes nothing, `T=<ttp>; $T <lookup>` lines, `ttp rollout status`, `ttp note --status`, `ttp upstream --note-state`, `ttp memory --search` and `:`/`continue`/`break` as read-only lookups, so 'no why recorded' lists only calls that change something.
