`tt-project`:

- The retired-rule echo notice no longer orders fixes when one coordinator decision is superseded by another that cites only a user message older than the retired decision (such as the source of a current Restrictions item). That supersede now counts as the coordinator's own, not the user withdrawing a rule, whether the cite is in the new text or its reason, on the user's turn or off it. A supersede citing a newer user message, or quoting or naming the user's words of that turn, still orders fixes.
- A current Restrictions item with its source now also covers a heading quoted with naming words ("the X restriction") or together with its item, and the items under a cited sub-heading. Such quotes in the charter, task specs or harness files are advisory when a memory entry's retirement matches them.
