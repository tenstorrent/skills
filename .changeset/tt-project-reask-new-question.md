`tt-project`: the coordinator's re-ask gate no longer refuses a new question about a settled request. When a question clause of the ask (a `?` sentence, from its last `:`, `;`, dash or `, which/should...` lead-in) brings at least two words the matched record lacks and the record holds under half of its words, the ask goes out. A verbatim or near-verbatim re-ask, or one that restates a correction with no new words, is still refused.
