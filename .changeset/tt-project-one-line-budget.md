`tt-project`: The web app's header and `ttp status` show the budget in exactly one line, for example
`5h 4% - resets in 3.9 h, 7d 21% - resets in 6.0 d, 24h $0.17 virtual, 5h avg 31%, 7d avg 72%`.
The windows and averages are the account's (mean of each completed window's peak: 5-hour windows over
7 days, weekly ones over 3 weeks); the dollars are this project's last 24 h, `virtual` on a plan and
`actual` when billed by use. Caps, limits and spend still running are in the Budget tab.
