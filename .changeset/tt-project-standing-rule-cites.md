`tt-project`: every coordinator (session and stateless, every provider) sees a model-free "Standing rules" index in its digest: charter Restrictions and Policies, standing memory, open user orders and holds set by the user or another person, one line each with a stable id (`R-…`, `P-…`, `M-…`, `o<n>`, `H-…`) and its message cite. The coordinator prompt asks it to cite a rule id, user msg, safety rule or the dependency it needs on every hold, deferral, wait, delivery route or held-resource action. A hold-type action that cites nothing is applied, flagged once in the next Course check (`uncited hold: …`) and counted; the daily Self-efficiency audit and `ttp audit` (`rule_cites`) show the share cited and the likely corrections that name a standing rule.
