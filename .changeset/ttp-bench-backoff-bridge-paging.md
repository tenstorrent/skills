Bench runs back off on their own when a model's server refuses over 20% of calls for capacity: to the probed safe level, else one step down until refusals are under 5%; running trials are never stopped, and `--rerun-server N` reruns only the tasks not scored. Every ssh-add the plugin runs or ships is bounded by a timeout. Bridge refresh restarts a session whose relay copy changed. A watcher's paged key is filed once and remembered for a day after its last report, or until it is reported cleared. The credential watch and the bridge status ignore the bridge's sockets and agent up-markers, which ssh rewrites in normal use.
