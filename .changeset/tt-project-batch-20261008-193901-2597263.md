`tt-project`: the charter guard finds the action of a lift with an inverted modal ('may workers push', 'may let workers push'), a passive helper ('may be done'), 'is also fine' or a bare 'is OK', and a lift naming no action ('Main is no longer off-limits'); actor words ('let', workers, agents, pronouns) are never a rule's target, so 'Never let workers X' pairs only with a lift of X; an inverted 'may' skips any actor before its verb ('may the coordinator push', 'may tasks push'); a ban on workers ('Workers must not push', 'Never let agents open PRs') does not pair with an allowance for another actor ('The coordinator may push'); an inverted 'may' skips a named actor ('may Dependabot push'); the charter guard makes no actor exemption; the lint pairs a ban on workers with an allowance that names workers anywhere ('The user may allow workers to push', '... and let workers do so too'); 'tell/have/ask workers to X' is X, and 'No worker may X' / 'Nobody may X' forbid X; the charter guard judges each clause of an appended sentence on its own, so a forbid in one clause never cancels an allowance in another ('No restriction remains and workers may push to main'); "No ... may" never reads across a clause join, and the guard counts a bare "can" as a permission; "No worker or agent may X" bans X (the guard's clause split keeps that subject whole, and the lint pairs it); the charter guard reads lifts said as no one minding ("No problem if", "No objection to", "No one objects when", "No rule stops ... from") as permissions and flags them; the "No X or Y may" subject join holds only when an actor noun heads the subject ("No worker or agent may", "No user or other bot may"), so "No review by a maintainer and agents may push to main" is flagged again; a "No problem if" lift whose clause negates the action stays a ban, and "No rule stops the coordinator or workers from ..." is read as a lift.
