The coordinator's charter guard accepts a scoped exception to a Restrictions ban: new Restrictions text sent with `excepts` (the clause it narrows) and the user's verbatim words as `source` passes when it names a scope the clause does not (hosts, resources, actions, a time window) and no other ban covers that scope. It is stored as its own item naming the clause and that it wins only inside its scope; the broader ban stays as written. General loosenings and exceptions without the user's own words are still refused. Every contradiction refusal now quotes each conflicting sentence with its section and a one-line reason.
