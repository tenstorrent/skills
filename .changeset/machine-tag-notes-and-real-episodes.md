`ttp machines tag-note <tag> "<text>"` keeps one note for every machine with a tag in the user's machines list; every coordinator sees it in its Machines section and workers see it for the machines their task names. A watcher's new episode after its key cleared is a real occurrence, no longer counted as noise. Model-bench cells are graded even when flagged (`heldout_passed`), the cell prompt names its time limit and a focused test command, and a Claude cell cut off before its result line is priced from its tokens instead of recording $0.
