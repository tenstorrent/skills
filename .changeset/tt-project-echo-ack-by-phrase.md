`tt-project`: a `ttp echoes --ack` now holds while its place holds the same phrases of that retirement, not while the matched lines are byte-for-byte unchanged. A note appended to a task spec, or an edit, that only re-quotes an acknowledged phrase keeps the ack; a new or changed phrase of the retirement lists the match again. Acks stored before this keep holding until their place changes.
