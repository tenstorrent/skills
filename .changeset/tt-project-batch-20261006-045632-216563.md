`tt-project`:

- ttp upgrade settles template conflicts that need no judgment and queues at most one upgrade task a day
- ttp ci, a CI wait probe that also wakes on a hung job (3x its recent median); ttp ci test docstring kept generic
- push queue waits delivery.push_min_gap_s (default 1800) after the last batch ended; the broken-tip hold test turns the push gap off
- Jev watcher screening prices a skipped wake at a low-effort turn, settles each skip against later wakes and tasks, and rare uses are judged after a full window
- close a code task left in review once its review's push lands, also after a conflict fix
