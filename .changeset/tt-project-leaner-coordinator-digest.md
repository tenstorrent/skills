`tt-project`: coordinator turns read a smaller digest.

- Recently finished tasks: the last 10, summaries cut to about 120 characters at a word; a task
  whose hand-off is among this turn's new events points there instead of repeating it.
- Recently sent messages are cut to about 100 characters; empty sections are left out.
- The coordinator's system prompt states the charter's restrictions once instead of twice.
- New events, open tasks, open questions and the budget are unchanged.
