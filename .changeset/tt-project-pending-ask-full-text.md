`tt-project`: a reminder of something waiting on the user quotes it in full. Open asks show their whole text in `ttp status`, `ttp overview`, the web board, wait text, the daily review and timeout notices, and a coordinator reply or notify naming an open ask only by its id gets the ask's full text appended (an unmatched "waiting on you" is flagged, never dropped).
