`tt-project`: the on-host status guard on coordinator replies and notifies marks only the sentences that claim the reply's unstarted host work is under way or done. A sentence naming no task #id or resource is tied to the reply's one unstarted task only when its subject is that work: the speaker ("I'm changing ..."), a pronoun ("It is running"), no subject ("Resetting the board") or a noun phrase sharing a word with the task's title. Sentences about other things ("The cut passed its review ...", "That batch failed two check passes and is now ...", "the provider stopped serving the model") stay as written, and a reply holding only facts and honest queue status is sent unchanged. The guard also reads "it's done" as a claim.
