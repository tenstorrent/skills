`tt-project`:

- obligations._result_file walks with an entry and time budget (scandir), not an unbounded glob; obligations._glob_walk joins literal leading parts ('out/', '..') directly and enters only folders that can still match, so a prefixed --results glob never spends its walk budget on unrelated folders
- the ask-answer linker leaves a reply to another outbound message ('re #N' in its text or relayer context, or in that message's thread) to that message, and links a broadcast ask's newest-rule answer only where the ask was delivered (Slack, once posted) or when the reply is about it; the ask-answer linker's reply-to claim ignores contractions (we're 5, they're 3), and a broadcast ask counts as delivered to a relay chat that read past it at or above its severity floor (notify.chat_min_severity applies; the web chat never counts)
