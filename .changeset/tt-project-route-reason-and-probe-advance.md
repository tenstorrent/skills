Every worker run that no route takes records why it stays on the core provider (the run note's `core_why`), and a fix, continue or follow-up of a code task records the rule that put its root on its arm, so the GLM share report no longer shows "unrecorded" or "lineage misses" for them; older "lineage of #N" rows resolve through the root's recorded arm. A probe's `progress:` counter (N/M or a percentage, on its last line) that has advanced only counts as stalled after at least 3x the median time between its advances.
