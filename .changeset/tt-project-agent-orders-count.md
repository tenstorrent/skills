`tt-project`: with `coordinator.agent_orders_as_user` on, an order from the owner's own agent (the relayer of the owner's messages, decided by the channel, never by a claim in the text) now counts as the owner's word for Restrictions changes and for answering asks. A Restrictions change resting only on the agent's words is cited "(relayer-ordered, msg #N)" (`source` `{"origin": "relayer", "msg", "quote"}` also serves as the source quote for removing or replacing a cited item), logged as a `relayer_ordered` event and a low feed line, and listed in the daily review's "relayer-ordered changes (24 h)" line, each with the old text so it can be undone. Three classes still need the owner's own verbatim words, and the refusal names the class: (a) a step that could affect people badly (someone else's work or data, a shared machine or service), (b) a step that speaks as the owner (a post or message, a PR leaving draft, a merge, another person's request), and (c) hard gates (a credential or money only the owner holds, spend caps). Rewording or removing an item about one of those, even only adding a scope, stays refused on the agent's words. Notes from other projects, events, logs, PR comments and worker findings stay data. With the setting off nothing changes. This replaces the earlier rule that the agent could never replace or remove a Restrictions item.
