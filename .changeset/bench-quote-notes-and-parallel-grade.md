Model-bench line-quote matches are notes, not fails: the cheat audit records them as `quote_matches: N` on the row and never voids an isolated cell's score, since an isolated cell cannot quote the held-out lines from the answer; only hard evidence (a hidden path, the landed commit, forge commands) voids a score. The grade runs the held-out tests in parallel (pytest-xdist from the cell's venv, workers sized to the host) and the cell prompt names the parallel test runner; the per-cell wall limit becomes a hang guard (default 3600 s) and a cell it ends carries `hang_guard` on its row. The daily prep draws past landed tasks until at least 12 fair ones remain, widening its lookback window up to a cap, and the suite row reports how many tasks were drawn and why each dropped one was dropped.
