`tt-project`: the coordinator and workers follow the intent of the user's orders and goals, not a literal reading; a Restriction still binds as written, its stricter reading in doubt. They decide on their own unless a step could hurt people, speak to people as the user or pass as a human. A rare, real ambiguity is asked in the reply while work goes on with the best reading, never waiting for the answer. With `coordinator.session` on, the coordinator's role is the project's hands-on manager: it knows what each running worker does right and wrong, corrects workers mid-run as orders come in, and fixes the work, the workers and the project's own prompts, scripts, charter notes and config itself under every gate, while builds, test suites, device runs and larger code changes still go to workers. The per-wake coordinator keeps its tool-less role. Both role prompts stay within their size budgets.
