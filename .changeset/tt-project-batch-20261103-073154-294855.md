`tt-project`: checks: a task's newer head in another worker run stops its running older-head checks and takes their place in the queue, so one task holds one checks lane; checks: a replaced run that could not be stopped keeps its place; checks: a task's older-head waiting checks with other commands yield their lane to its newer head, which takes their place in the queue; checks: a superseded follower records and reports only the commands its lead ran, names its own that did not run, and leaves the lead's checks.json in the same run folder as is.
