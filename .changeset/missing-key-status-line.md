`tt-project`: a missing cheap-model or model-ranking key is a status line only, never a "needs you" item or a sign-in ask, even for work pinned to the chat provider. Saving the key resumes both by themselves: the provider's login check runs at once on the changed key store and routing returns to it, and the ranking refresh, which retries every few minutes, picks the key up on its next try. Neither needs a coordinator turn.
