Missing entries and promise slack: a worker whose user-ordered step a guard or allowlist refuses notes
`missing-entry: <setting or file> | <entry> | order #<id>`, which wakes the coordinator at once with a
must-act item to add the entry and wake or requeue the task, unless a Restriction, a user hold or a
user-only setting forbids it. A promise due more than 15 minutes out for under 10 minutes of work, or
whose due moved twice before its work started, is a must-act item until the work starts or the
coordinator justifies the wait.
