Corrections: a `#<n>` list after a PR, issue, repo or message word is no task anchor also with
punctuation or up to two plain words between ('PR: #7, #8', 'PR carrying #7'), and a `#<n>` naming an
outbound reply of the same chat is none either. 'task #<n>', 'review #<n>' and 'the PR for #<n>' still
anchor.
