`tt-project`: the routine coordinator lane hands more wakes to the coordinator's own session and repeats itself less. A wake goes to the coordinator when an open promise is due within the hour or overdue, when a task that owns a promise finishes, or when a due look is tied to an order, a promise or a chat. A lane answer of only noops and looks on a wake with a must-act item or a stall, run-cap or unread-steer event is an escalation. In shadow mode the lane prompt lists its own recent proposals the coordinator did not take on the same wake, and the prompt adds a short check list before an idle answer. `ttp stats --coord-routine` also shows how often an escalation was right: the coordinator's own answer needed an action outside the lane.
