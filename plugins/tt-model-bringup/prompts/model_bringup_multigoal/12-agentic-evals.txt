/goal Use $agentic-serving and $tt-device-usage for HF_MODEL after the benchmark stage in the current tt-metal checkout.

Subagents are authorized when a selected skill requests or recommends them.

This goal runs agentic evals on the served autoport and tunes its serving configuration for them, following the goal-12 section of $agentic-serving. Read bringup/agentic_evals/inputs.json if it exists; otherwise use that skill's defaults. Use only the hardware the inputs grant, and keep it busy.

Goal completion requirements:
- Eval names and versions, agent and request time limits and the output cap come from the release spec, never below its values; each eval is served on the smallest validated batch at or above its planned clients.
- Before any scored sample, doc/agentic_evals/survey.json answers every check and calculation in the skill's survey, prices each shortfall carried from earlier stages in suite hours, and doc/agentic_evals/preflight.json shows the registry, disk, release route and an isolated smoke passing.
- doc/agentic_evals/efforts.json lists every problem the survey found with its predicted suite hours saved per eval, hours of work, the basis of each (guesses are allowed and marked), and how it will confirm success without the full suites. Each ranking orders by saving per hour of work, with a recorded reason for any effort moved out of that order, and is redone after every effort. Each effort's sub-agent is given its time box, writes its own goal and plan to fit inside it, updates the plan as data arrives, and finishes as soon as its confirmation settles. Efforts run in parallel inside the effort budget, try the fastest test first, and record the change (kept, dropped or none) and the problem (solved or open) with the confirmed result and hours used. Exact changes are accepted on a paired exactness check plus their confirmation; others need a scored pair against the current best. Kept changes are re-checked together.
- doc/agentic_evals/estimate.json records per eval a go/stop decision and reason, first from the survey and updated after each effort from the calculations, not from full suites. Full suites run only on go and record actual hours next to the prediction. A measured stop is a valid outcome.
- doc/agentic_evals/serving_config.json records the measured serving values a release spec needs, and doc/agentic_serving.json `workload` is updated with measured values.
- doc/agentic_evals/REPORT.md leads with hours per suite and scores against references, then each effort's predicted saving and work hours against its confirmed result and hours used, the chosen configuration and remaining limits. Scores are reported, not gated.
- Only owned processes and containers are stopped. Commit stage-owned changes to the execution checkout locally and log SHAs; never push that checkout or add a remote to it.

Unmet requirements and failed gates: work. Use $autofix for execution or integration failures. Stop only after `$autofix` fails or the stage budget ends, preserving partial results and the reason.
