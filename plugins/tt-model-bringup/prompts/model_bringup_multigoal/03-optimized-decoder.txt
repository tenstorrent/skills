/goal Use $optimize and $tt-device-usage for the HF_MODEL fused decoder in the current tt-metal checkout.

Subagents are authorized when a selected skill requests or recommends them.

This goal creates the optimized-decoder state for the repo-local TTNN autoport pipeline. Start from the completed fused decoder and work only on models/autoports/<model>/tt/optimized_decoder.py plus its tests and docs; do not begin multichip-decoder, full-model, or vLLM work in this goal.

Goal completion requirements:
- models/autoports/<model>/tt/optimized_decoder.py exists and the delivered tests exercise the optimized path, not a functional fallback.
- The optimized decoder preserves the fused decoder's prefill/decode semantics, paged KV-cache behavior, determinism, and representative layer-kind coverage.
- Preserve the fused decoder's context contract; if layout, sharding, or KV-cache dtype changes capacity, update doc/context_contract.json. Reduce advertised capability only for a hard physical limit (e.g. DRAM), with evidence of the largest feasible value.
- The optimized decoder preserves support for valid non-aligned logical sequence lengths; internal padding/chunking is fine, public `seq_len % chunk_size == 0` restrictions are not.
- Prefill and decode PCC remain at the functional acceptance bar for every meaningful layer kind, with any material delta explained.
- Warmed prefill and traced warmed decode latency are reported before and after optimization.
- Final code must beat the best correct traced-decode baseline/candidates; fewer ops or cleaner topology is not enough.
- Weights stay BFP8 by default. If MLP/expert matmuls dominate decode, measure BFP4/LoFi as a candidate (real weights, perf rows); stage 8 $datatype-sweep and its precision probe make the final choice.
- tt-perf-report tables/CSV exist for prefill and decode. Try actionable advice; rejected advice has before/after evidence. A first TTNN/API error is not enough to reject a material op, reshard, layout, or host-fallback optimization.
- The optimization pass starts with an operation-topology audit of the measured path: repeated same-input matmuls, avoidable reshard/layout conversions, candidate fused/packed projections, optimized composite ops, and lower-movement replacements. The README or work log records current ops, candidate replacements, action taken, and evidence.
- The optimization pass addresses model-applicable decoder opportunities now: canonical precision/fidelity policy, sharded layouts, fused or packed same-input projections, DRAM-sharded decode matmuls, large prefill program configs, SDPA/composite ops, memory/program/compute-kernel configs, runtime data movement, and MoE active-expert execution. Completed and rejected options have evidence. Keep packed/fused same-input projections only when they beat a well-tuned legal separate candidate or the separate candidate is invalid.
- The final optimized runtime path has no unnecessary torch/from_torch/to_torch, tilize/untilize, reshard, or host fallback in measured prefill/decode.
- Stress or repeated-run coverage and a watcher-clean optimized correctness run exist.
- models/autoports/<model>/doc/optimized_decoder/README.md and work_log.md record commands, before/after PCC and perf, chosen configs, tt-perf-report conclusions, rejected options, limitations, and exact artifacts.
- $optimize checklist items are assessed with evidence until the $model-bringup stop rule: within 1.5x of roofline, 3 candidates in a row under 3% gain, or the 8 h time box. The work log lists untried items and why; quality gates are unchanged.
- $stage-review returns `clean-pass`; findings fixed/rereviewed. Local commit stage-owned touched-repo changes; never push; log SHAs.

Unmet requirements, review findings, failed gates: work. Stop only after `$autofix` fails.
